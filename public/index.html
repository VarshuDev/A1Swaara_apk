What's New in v1.0.2

  • Enabled R8 shrinking and resource optimization, reducing APK size from ~28.8 MB to 12.8 MB.
  • Fixed returning-user cold startup: skips blocking splash and moves heavy initialization off the main thread.
  • Fixed language onboarding singer portraits to load live YouTube Music artist artwork with neon monogram fallbacks.
  • Kept Mini-Player and 5-tab Navigation Bar persistent across Album, Artist, Playlist, and Mood detail screens.
  • Upgraded Now Playing to a spring-animated drag sheet with swipe-up Mini-Player open and swipe-down dismiss.
  • Added Stale-While-Revalidate disk caching on Home with parallel fetches and non-destructive offline retry states.
  • Added live Moods & Genres browse grid, loading shimmers, deletable recent searches, and Retry UI in Search.
  • Added actionable Retry and Skip Next recovery buttons on playback errors and one-tap crash recovery diagnostics.
